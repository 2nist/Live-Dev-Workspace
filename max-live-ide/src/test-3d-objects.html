Key Improvements

  • High Contrast Text: White text with dark background and text shadows for maximum readability

  • Interactive Feedback: Hover and selection states enhance user experience
  • Color-Coded Categories: Audio, MIDI, Live API, and Utility objects have distinct styling
  • Enhanced Handles: 3D circular connection points with gradient effects
